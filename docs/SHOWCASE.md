# Nyuu Showcase 055

[Download the showcase ZIP](../downloads/NyuuTerraria-Showcase.zip) · [Player guide](../README.md)

Install the matching **v0.5.5** mod first, then close the game.

1. Copy the two files in **Players** to `Documents/My Games/Terraria/tModLoader-dev/Players`.
2. Copy the two files in **Worlds** to `Documents/My Games/Terraria/tModLoader-dev/Worlds`.
3. Keep each `.plr` with its `.tplr`, and each `.wld` with its `.twld`. Local saves only; no cloud transfer is needed.

Select **Nyuu Showcase 055** for both player and world.

## What's inside

* All 146 mod items in labeled category chests, plus 45 vanilla crafting ingredients and every crafting station.
* A **decoration gallery** on the ground floor: the Prism Wardrobe, all six memorials, the Discovery Plaque, vases, the Amber Urn and the Reach Lantern.
* Mod furniture, Lyra and Mira, all spell modes learned and every profession leveled.
* Endgame test gear on the character. Use the early-game equipment in the chests to judge progression balance.
* World progression already unlocked, so you can visit every destination and rematch bosses. Quests are still open to try.

## Destinations

* **The Reverie is its own dimension.** Use the **Garden Mirror** (hotbar slot 3). The first visit generates the Reverie world, so expect a short loading screen. Use the mirror inside it to come home.
* **The Hush, Tidepool, Citadel, Cinderwood and Starglass** are still pocket regions in this world, reached with their travel items on the hotbar.

Hotbar: Aetherloom Wand, Field Journal, Garden Mirror, Hush portal, Tide Compass, Citadel Sigil, Cinder Key, Starglass Chart, Celestial Loom and Zenith.

Original characters and worlds are not part of this ZIP and are not changed by importing these uniquely named files. This is a disposable test save, not a balanced survival start. Back up a fresh copy before destructive bomb testing.
