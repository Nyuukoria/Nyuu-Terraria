## Controls and first steps

Default keys: **L** Chronicle, **K** Spellbook, **V** Backpack, **G** profession ability. Existing assigned keys stay assigned; change any binding in Settings → Controls → Mod Controls. Escape closes the Chronicle. Its close button also works.

Craft a **Field Journal** from a Book and 2 Silk at a Work Bench, or use L. Its Worldbound tab contains Requests, Talents, Profile, Wildlife, Stories, and Workshop. Pin an objective in Requests; collect and claim its reward in Quests. Completed objectives stay completed after rejoining.

## Six destinations, one persistent world

These are **pocket expeditions within your current world**, with separate terrain, landmarks, threats, and finite loot. They are not separate world files or full-size dimensions. Existing Reverie and Hush remain available. Four new travel items open the locations below. Use the same key again to return. Travel is blocked during boss fights. New areas only occupy unused sky space and do not replace your builds.

| Destination | Access | Find and do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tidepool Hollows | Tide Compass; available early | Tide Pearls, Reef Skitterers, Tidepool Turtles, a tide shrine, a finite loot chest, and the Reef Ambusher miniboss. |
| Overgrown Citadel | Citadel Sigil; available early | Pale Blooms, Vinebound Armor, Garden Birds, a garden landmark and chest. Summon the Pale Gardener here. |
| Cinderwood Depths | Cinder Key; defeat Skeletron | Cinder Blooms, Ash Burrowers, Lantern Fireflies, charred trees, a forge landmark and chest. |
| Starglass Expanse | Starglass Chart; defeat Moon Lord | Starglass Shards, Starfallen Constructs, Crystal Snails, a celestial landmark and chest. Start the Starlit Convergence after preparing. |

See [the recipe reference](ITEMS.md) for each travel item's exact ingredients. Defeated bosses and discovered areas save with the world. Trees now have branching crowns; already-built expeditions retain their terrain so player changes survive. Use a fresh world or the showcase to see the new scenery immediately.

## Magic workshop

| Addition | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| Element reactions | Aetherloom spell hits mark an enemy for 4 seconds. Ember + Tide triggers Steam, Frost + Tide triggers Frozen Bloom, Storm + Tide triggers Charged Tide. Each enemy has a 1-second reaction cooldown. Marks belong to the caster. |
| Ritual Focus | Consumable circle, 25 mana. A visible 90-tick windup precedes damage pulses; only one active circle per player. Craft 3 from 2 Moonprism Bars + 2 Fallen Stars at an anvil. |
| Cauldron Workshop | Place it, stand nearby, and open crafting. Right-click opens the inventory, rather than the Chronicle. Brew Bottled Water + Daybloom + a destination material. |
| Infusions | Tide: mana regeneration; Cinder: life regeneration; Starglass: magic critical chance. Each lasts 3 minutes. Drinking another replaces the previous infusion. |
| Celestial Loom | 105 base magic damage, 14 mana, 28-tick casting, piercing shots and five arcane fragments. A post-Moon Lord craft; full recipe in the reference. |
| Spell Palette | Use to cycle four spell colors. Saved per character. Colors change appearance, not damage. |

The original Aetherloom casting modes, healing, shield, mastery, and learning tomes remain in [Spellcraft](../README.md#spellcraft).

## Professions and talents

Change your active profession freely in the Chronicle. **Every profession keeps its own XP, level and purchased ranks**. Only the active branch applies its bonuses. Gain one talent point every 3 profession levels; each talent has 3 ranks. Reset a branch for free. Ability effects last 5 seconds with a 30-second cooldown; an ability talent must have a rank before G works.

| Profession | First talent, per rank | Second talent, per rank | G ability |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Vanguard | +2 defense | +2% melee speed | Regeneration |
| Ranger | +1 ranged crit | 5% ammo conservation | Swiftness |
| Arcanist | +1 magic crit | +10 maximum mana | Magic Power |
| Summoner | +2% summon damage | +1 minion at rank 3 | Ironskin |
| Explorer | +2% movement speed | +2% mining speed | Spelunker |

At profession level 15, claim a Mastery Crest in Talents once per profession. Its damage bonus scales with the active profession, capped at 6%. Original profession upgrades remain separate from these talent points. Profile identity and story labels are optional character flavor and save per character.

## Stories, discoveries and rewards

There are **26 quests** including 14 new Worldbound requests, plus four choice scenes. Start with the original atelier quests, then continue Lyra's missing-pattern and coastal/garden chapters. The Hush continues through archive discoveries; Mira has memorial requests. Prerequisites unlock subsequent chapters. Story choices record progress once per scene; revisiting does not duplicate quest credit.

Noticeboards open Requests. Landmark discoveries are recorded when you visit them; the journal can pin one objective on screen. Rewards include the Coastal Coat, Garden Dress, Heritage Ribbon and other equipment. The Heritage Ribbon grants mana and regeneration. ## Wildlife, home and memorials

Observe all eight peaceful species nearby with a clear line of sight: **Dew Fox, Glass Deer, Mothcat, Mooncap Sprite** in the original expedition habitats; **Tidepool Turtle, Garden Bird, Lantern Firefly, Crystal Snail** in the four new destinations. The Wildlife page records sightings and feeding. Claim the Explorer Medallion after all eight: mining/movement bonuses and treasure sight.

Put a **Feeding Bowl** near a peaceful creature and right-click it with a Daybloom in your inventory. It consumes one flower, heals the nearest eligible creature and encourages it to follow for a minute. A nearby **Pet Bed** heals creatures and lets unfed creatures rest. Wild creatures remain world NPCs; the existing summoned pets remain separate items. The Garden Lantern emits light. Beds and ordinary decoration do not open the journal.

**Mira, the Cemetery Keeper**, can move into suitable housing after the Eye of Cthulhu is defeated. Use Memorial stories to open her story page. Craft Tidal, Cinder and Starglass Memorials at a Work Bench; the full material counts are in the reference. Death in the new areas drops a themed memorial item you can place yourself; it does not replace vanilla grave generation. Memorial Wreaths and lanterns help build a remembrance garden.

Four new outfit pairs — Coastal, Cinder, Starglass and Garden — craft at a Loom from Silk and destination materials. Equip them in vanity slots. The worn sheets include collars, cuffs, fastenings and animated hems; the existing Warden, Cloudtail and Prism garments also received the visual pass.

## Events

**Garden Festival:** a peaceful five-minute visitor event every third dawn, or begin it with a Garden Festival Bell (5 Pale Blooms + 5 Daybloom at a Work Bench). It increases the new peaceful creature spawn chance. **Starlit Convergence:** an optional three-minute Starglass event after Moon Lord, started with a Starlit Beacon (10 Starglass Shards + 2 Luminite Bars at an Ancient Manipulator); more constructs appear. Events save with the world.

## Extending the stories

Add JSON packs under `NyuuTerraria/Data/packs/`, with unique quest and scene IDs. Packs can add prerequisite quests, event objectives, two-choice dialogue scenes and replies. Rebuild the mod after editing. See [content authoring](CONTENT-AUTHORING.md). Large branching story graphs, full-size dimensions, expanded event chapters and advanced class abilities remain in the roadmap.
