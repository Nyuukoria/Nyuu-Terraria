# Creatures and companions

[Home](../README.md) · [Destinations](DESTINATIONS.md) · [Wildlife and home](WORLDBOUND.md#wildlife-home-and-memorials)

v0.5.2 animates the existing artwork for **33 custom NPC types and six pet/minion projectile types**. Their original designs are preserved; movement is applied to sprite parts rather than replacing them with a different art style.

| Animation group | Inhabitants | Movement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Town | Lyra, Cemetery Keeper | Existing walking frames, cloth and hair movement; grounded feet. |
| Quadruped | Dew Fox, Dew Fox Pet, Glass Deer, Mothcat, Tidepool Turtle, Mossback Beetle, Glass Crawler, Reef Ambusher, Reef Skitterer, Reefback Guardian | Alternating leg motion while moving, plus tail movement. |
| Moth | Lantern Moth, Glasswing Moth, Prism Matriarch, Pride Moth Pet | Wing flaps around body joints; faster airborne rhythm. |
| Bird | Garden Bird, Glowcap Firefly, Lantern Firefly | Wing motion and subtle tail movement. |
| Serpent | Ash Burrower, Glass Serpent, Crystal Snail | A traveling ripple through the body. |
| Bell | Mourning Bell, Bellkeeper, Little Glass Bell | Swinging lower bell and clapper. |
| Spirit | Nimbus Warden, Paper Wraith, Lantern Stalker, The Mirrorless, Amber Wisp | Flowing lower silhouette and idle movement. |
| Crystal | Prism Sentinel, Starfallen Construct | Moving outer shards and a pulsing core. |
| Mushroom | Mooncap Sprite, Mooncap Minion | Subtle cap expansion and contraction. |
| Humanoid | Rootbound Knight, Pale Gardener, The Unwritten, Archive Caretaker, Vinebound Armor | Arm and leg movement, with a stronger pose during attacks. |

## Discover and care for wildlife

Open the Chronicle's Wildlife page to follow observations. Peaceful creatures belong to their respective destinations; the [Worldbound guide](WORLDBOUND.md#wildlife-home-and-memorials) explains observations, pets, beds and feeding bowls. Pet furnishings are crafted items, listed individually in the [recipe catalog](ITEMS.md).

Animations are visual; they do not change health, drops, hitboxes or progression. Grounded creatures retain collision and gravity, and flying companions keep their own movement rules.
