## Settle in

Open **L → Requests** and hand in 8 Wood for Lyra's first request. Pin a request to track it, then press **Claim** when ready. Follow its prerequisite chain; completed requests cannot be claimed twice.

Start storage with **12 Copper Bars + any chest** at an anvil. Equip the Copper Backpack on the Equipment / pets page and press **V**. Upgrades consume the preceding backpack and require empty backpack storage. [All six tiers](PLAYER-GUIDE.md#backpacks).

Explore normal underground stone for Skylight, deeper caverns for Stormglass, snow for Frostveil, Amber Grottos for Verdigris and Ember Amber, and Hollowglass Reach for its own resources. Some ores require a newly created world. [Material locations](PLAYER-GUIDE.md#where-to-find-materials).

## Magic and builds

Democloud equipment starts the magic route: the staff recipe uses **25 Demonite Bars + 50 Cloud + 15 Wood**. The wings use **20 Cloud + 10 Demonite Bars**. Consult the [recipe catalog](ITEMS.md) for stations and every alternative.

Progress through Skylight, Amber and expedition equipment. The **Aetherloom Wand** opens elemental casting, learnable modes and saved mastery; press **K** for the Spellbook. [Spellcraft instructions](PLAYER-GUIDE.md#spellcraft).

Combine Ember/Tide, Frost/Tide or Storm/Tide marks for reactions. Ritual Focus adds delayed area pulses; the Cauldron Workshop brews destination infusions. Celestial Loom is a post-Moon Lord weapon. [Workshop and reactions](WORLDBOUND.md#magic-workshop).

Professions can be switched freely. Each retains its XP and purchased ranks; only the active profession applies its bonuses. Talents add active abilities, and level 15 unlocks a mastery reward. [Five profession branches](WORLDBOUND.md#professions-and-talents).

## Explore and prepare

Craft a Garden Mirror for the Reverie. Gather Moonprism, begin expedition quests and prepare for its encounters. The Hush requires a Cracked Bell Portal and offers a darker branch with configurable scares. Tidepool and Citadel are early destinations, Cinderwood requires Skeletron, and Starglass requires Moon Lord.

Use the same travel item again to return. Boss fights block travel; bring healing, mana and mobility before summoning.
