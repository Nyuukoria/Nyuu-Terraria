# v0.5.6 — The Hush, and art that looks like Terraria

## The Hush is its own world

In single player the **Cracked Bell Portal** now opens a separate world, like the Reverie:

* an **abandoned mining town** of boarded-up shacks and lampposts,
* a **mine shaft** with a rope down to the **forgotten archive**, two galleries of bookshelves,
* a three-floor **manor** on the eastern ridge, where the Cracked Bell Sigil waits upstairs.

Use the portal inside the Hush to come back where you left. Multiplayer keeps the pocket region.

## Art rebuilt on Terraria's own sprites

Everything below now uses a vanilla sprite as its base (shape, shading, bevels, veins and edges),
recoloured into Nyuu palettes with hue-shifted shading: cool shadows, warm highlights.

* **Bars and ores:** all seven bars and ore items, and each material's tile, bar and ore item come
  from the same vanilla family, so they match. Skylight uses Mythril, Stormglass Adamantite,
  Frostveil Cobalt, Verdigris Chlorophyte, Ember Amber Palladium and gold, Hollowglass Titanium,
  Moonprism Orichalcum and Luminite.
* **Ore blocks, Moss Slate, Glass Slate and its wall** are vanilla tile sheets recoloured, with every
  edge and dirt-blend frame. Dirt pixels stay real dirt.
* **Stormglass** is purple everywhere now. Its tile used to be teal while the item and map were purple.
* **Garden Mirror** is built on the Magic Mirror; **Prism Cocoon** on a crystal shard.
* **Decorations:** the six memorials are tombstones on a plinth. The cauldron, garden lantern, amber
  urn, glass vase, discovery plaque, noticeboard and pet bed are built on vanilla furniture.

## Fixes

* **Inventory icons:** the Discovery Plaque and the Tidal, Cinder and Starglass memorials showed the
  Memorial Wreath icon. Each has its own now, and every decoration's icon matches what it places.
* **Reloading the mod leaked memory:** the portal sky stayed registered in Terraria after unload,
  which kept the old copy of the mod alive ("mod class still using memory"). It's removed on unload.
* **Startup:** the title logo no longer blocks loading.
* **Multiplayer Reverie pocket:** placement searched one row of 18 spots and often found no room
  ("No clear sky space was found"). It now searches four sky rows densely, like the other destinations.

## Checks

The headless suite passes all 597 checks, and the new dimension test generates, saves and reloads
both the Reverie and the Hush.
