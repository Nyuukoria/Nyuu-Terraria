# v0.5.3 — Living Worlds polish

A presentation release. No saves break, no recipes change, no balance numbers move.

## Companions

All six pets and the mooncap minion were drawing frame 0 of a four-frame sheet forever, and were
cut into moving joints on top of that. They now play the four authored poses and are anchored on
their artwork instead of their frame box.

| Companion | Frames before | Frames now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Glowcap Firefly | 2 of 4 distinct | 4 |
| Amber Wisp | 2 of 4 | 4 |
| Little Glass Bell | 2 of 4 | 4 |
| Pride Moth | 2 of 4 | 4 |
| Dew Fox | 3 of 4 | 4 |
| Mooncap Sprite | 3 of 4 | 4 |

## Outfits

The seven coats shared one silhouette across six of them, and five of the seven leg pieces shared
another — the same art recoloured. Each piece now carries rim light on the upper-left edge, a soft
edge shade, and one accent that is its own: a cuff band, a collar band, a hem band, a centre seam,
a pauldron line. Sheet sizes are untouched, so nothing moved on the character.

## Backpacks

Shift + left click an inventory item while the backpack is open: it stacks onto a matching item
first, then lands in the first free slot. Nothing was bound to that combination before, so no
existing shortcut changed.

## Size

Nine bosses were painted at 102–124 px inside hitboxes they already occupied — large enough to
fight, small on screen. They are drawn 1.20–1.35× larger, so the sprite now matches the body.
Seven creatures got the same treatment, led by Vinebound Armor (22×32 of art in a 38×38 body).

Hitboxes, damage, health and drops are unchanged.
