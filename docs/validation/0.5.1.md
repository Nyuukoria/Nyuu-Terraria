# v0.5.1 repair validation

The repair build passes **476 automated assertions**, including the existing content, spellcraft, travel and equipment checks. New checks cover quest hand-ins from backpack slot 100, exact material consumption, duplicate-claim rejection, ground-versus-flying enemy movement, vanilla's actual equipment-page selector, and removal of wall-to-wall expedition scaffolding. The lightning-chain test now explicitly starts in clear sky, rather than allowing newly generated trees to obstruct its line of sight.

The actual game log identified the backpack exception: `UILinkPointNavigator.SetPosition(440)` from drawing a bank context beyond its 40-slot navigation range. Each custom storage slot now draws through a single-slot view. Backpack cursor routing hooks the return from `ItemSlot.SelectEquipPage`, after vanilla's automatic selection, rather than relying on an earlier UI update.

Ready requests now expose Claim on the tracking page. Journal clicks are captured before vanilla inventory drawing can consume them. Materials can be handed in from inventory or backpack; completed requests reject further claims.

The established artwork is retained. Town sheets are oriented consistently and grounded; furniture is aligned to its bottom tile row; clothing uses the original animated leg masks instead of a static stretched item icon. The single-frame boomerang no longer advances through nonexistent animation frames. Ground enemies and the fox companion have gravity and collision.

Fresh expedition generation and the showcase exporter complete successfully: 145 mod items, 44 vanilla ingredients, nine catalog chests and six separate destinations, with a player-file reload check. Existing player-built expedition areas are not regenerated.
