# v0.5.8 — Outfits and motion, redone

## Outfits rebuilt on Terraria's own vanity

All seven sets are now built on vanilla vanity body and leg sheets, recoloured into each set's
palette with Terraria-style shading: coats with lapels and buttons, real dress skirts and trousers.

| Set | Built on |
| --- | --- |
| Cloudtail Coat and Trousers | a pastel coat and slim trousers |
| Warden Mantle and Greaves | lavender pauldron armour and armoured greaves |
| Cindercoat and Cinder Skirt | a dark coat with ember lapels and a flared skirt |
| Coastal Coat and Skirt | a buttoned coat and a long teal skirt |
| Garden Dress and Skirt | a white-collared dress and a full skirt |
| Starglass Coat and Skirt | a collared coat and a wide skirt |
| Prism Longcoat and Skirt | a lavender top and a layered skirt |

The inventory icons now show the garment you actually wear.

## Animation, redone the Terraria way

The v0.5.7 motion squashed wings and slid whole leg bands, which looked off. Now:

* **Moths and butterflies** fold their wings in toward the body and open them again, the way
  Terraria's own butterflies and moths animate.
* **Walkers and knights:** each leg is found separately, and alternate legs lift in turn instead of
  the body skating.
* **Spirits, birds, fireflies and the Mooncap** float with a gentle bob; bells swing; serpents
  ripple; crystals pulse.

## Fixes

* **Crescent Ward and Emberbloom charge at 0 mana:** Terraria 1.4.5's new mana rules let the mana
  check pass on an empty pool, so the ward could be held up, and a charge kept going, forever. Both
  now stop when the mana isn't there; a Mana Flower can still drink a potion first.
* **Test suite:** the ward check wiped the wand from the inventory when it wasn't in slot 0.

The headless suite passes all 597 checks.
