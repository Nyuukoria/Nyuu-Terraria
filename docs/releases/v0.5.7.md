# v0.5.7 — Creatures that move, weapons that shine

## Real animation for creatures and pets

All 35 creature and pet sheets have real motion now. Before this, their four frames were nearly
identical, so they barely moved. Each type moves in its own way:

* **Moths and butterflies** (Lantern Moth, Glasswing Moth, Prism Matriarch, Pride Moth pet) flap
  their wings, and beat faster than other creatures cycle.
* **Walkers** (Dew Fox, Glass Deer, Mothcat, turtles, beetles, crawlers, Reefback Guardian) step
  with front and back legs swinging opposite ways, and the body bobs.
* **Knights and humanoids** lift alternate legs; **serpents** ripple; **the snail** stretches its foot.
* **Bells** swing; **spirits** float with trailing cloth; **crystals** bob and pulse.
* **Birds and fireflies** hover smoothly.

The art and hitboxes are unchanged. Only the four frames in each sheet are new.

## Weapons and artifacts

* **Rebuilt on matching Terraria items:** Echo Scythe (Death Sickle), Staff of Democloud (Nimbus
  Rod), Moonprism Bow, Skylight Saber, Field Journal, Chronicle Journal, Nyuu Spellbook, Ritual
  Focus, Rematch Sigil, Wings of Democloud (icon and worn wings), and Yuuki's Anti-Fall-Damage Boots.
* **Kept your designs, cleaned up:** Aetherloom Wand, Dawnpetal Staff, Stormweaver Staff,
  Hollowchime Staff, Prism Moth Staff, Stormglass Scepter and Mooncap Scepter got a tidier palette,
  less speckle, and a coloured outline.
