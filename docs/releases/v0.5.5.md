# v0.5.5 — Worlds of their own

The Reverie becomes a real dimension, and the art that looked off is rebuilt: outfits, ores, slate
blocks, walls and decorations. New showcase save included.

## The Reverie is its own world

In single player the **Garden Mirror** now takes you to a separate world, like the Nether, instead
of a pocket in your sky. Nothing is built in your home world.

* The first visit generates the Reverie (a short loading screen). It's a 1600×900 world with flower
  meadows, moonlit lakes, floating flower islands, Moonprism caves and an abandoned observatory.
* Use the mirror inside the Reverie to save it and come back to exactly where you left.
* Each home world gets its own Reverie, stored in `Worlds/NyuuDimensions/<world id>/` so it never
  appears in the world list.
* Hardmode and boss progress from your home world carries in, so gates match your game.
* Multiplayer and older saves with a Reverie sky pocket keep working as before.
* Dimensions are built by the mod's own small subworld pipeline rather than vanilla world
  generation, which assumes a full-size world. Only the dimension's own generator runs, behind
  its own loading screen, and the result is saved like any other world.

## Art fixes

* **Outfit sleeves:** the icon pass had pasted a shrunken copy of each coat into every arm frame, so
  arms drew as tiny floating coats. Arms are real sleeves again, coloured from each coat. Torsos keep
  the new designs, cleaned to a small palette with an outline.
* **Legs:** no longer near-black. They take their coat's colours; the walk cycle is unchanged.
* **Ores, Moss Slate, Glass Slate:** every frame now uses Terraria's exact block shapes, including
  the frames where a block meets dirt, so veins have proper edges instead of square boxes. Ore shows
  as shaded crystals in stone or slate.
* **Glass Slate Wall:** rebuilt at full size with vanilla wall edges; it had invisible holes.
* **Decorations:** the Prism Wardrobe, Cauldron Workshop, Garden Lantern, Cinder Memorial and Reach
  Lantern are redrawn. The others are cleaned up, and every decoration now sits on the ground; the
  wardrobe, three memorials and the Reverie Vase used to float above it. The Prism Wardrobe has its
  own item icon instead of reusing the Garden Mirror.

## Other fixes

* **Backpack quick stash:** favourited items and backpacks are no longer stashed, stacking follows
  tModLoader's rules, and it plays the grab sound.
* **Nyuu Test Kit:** drops sync in multiplayer; tooltip added.
* `icon_small.png` added.
