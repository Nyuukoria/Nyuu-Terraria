# v0.5.4 — Wear what the icon shows

Presentation release. No saves break, no recipes change, no balance numbers move.

## Outfits are rebuilt from their icons

The inventory icon for each garment is drawn at twice the worn resolution — a 40 px icon for a
20 px torso. That means the icon *is* the garment, just bigger. Every `_Body` and `_Legs` sheet is
now generated by halving its icon with a 2×2 mode reduction (which keeps the icon's own palette)
and fitting the result into each frame's own pose box, so:

* lapels, the open front and the flared hem are now visible on the character
* the torso is replaced, not painted over — nothing of the old barrel survives there
* pixels the icon does not cover are filled from the icon's own edge colour, so no skin shows
  through where the coat is narrower than the old shape
* arms and side views still land where the pose puts them: outside the torso column the pose
  keeps its shape and takes the icon's sleeve tone
* legs are recoloured, not re-shaped, because the walk swing lives in their alpha — 11 of 20
  distinct leg frames kept against 14 before, rather than the 3 a silhouette swap produced
* the frame that only holds a hand or a hip patch is left untouched
* sheet sizes are unchanged — 360×224 and 40×1120 — and no frame lost a painted pixel

## Title screen

`Content/Title/TitleBackdrop.png` and `Content/Title/TitleLogo.png` are drawn over the vanilla
menu, with `Assets/Music/NyuuTitle.wav` looping underneath — an A-minor pad, bass and arpeggio at
66 BPM, 42 seconds, seamless loop. Both halves fail soft: if `Main.DrawMenu` is ever renamed the
menu stays vanilla, and the music is one int that vanilla already reads.

## Facing

Several creatures are painted facing left, which Terraria's mirror convention assumes is right.
Those seven — DewFox, DewFoxPet, GlassDeer, Mothcat, GardenBird, GlassSerpent, ReefbackGuardian —
now mirror the other way and walk forwards.

## Backpack

Shift + left click now has to be a real click: shift held first, then the button pressed. Clicking
first and pressing shift no longer stashes anything.

## Earlier in 0.5.x

Pets play all four frames, bosses and small creatures are drawn at their body size, and the ore and
slate tiles are on the 54×54 grid.

## Post-release fixes

* **Outfit sleeves** — the icon pass had pasted a shrunken copy of each coat into every arm and
  shoulder frame, so arms drew as tiny floating coats. Arm frames are back to real sleeve shapes,
  recoloured from each coat's own palette. Torsos keep the new designs, reduced to a 7-colour
  palette with a dark outline so they read as pixel art instead of a scaled painting.
* **Legs** — the last pass had dulled every leg piece to near-black. They now take their coat's
  colours again; the walk cycle (14 distinct frames) is untouched.
* **Ores** — every frame of the seven ore sheets was a full square, so veins showed as hard boxes.
  The sheets are now proper 288×270 frame sheets with outlined, rounded exposed edges, and the ore
  shows as shaded crystals set in a stone or slate bed.
* **Moss Slate / Glass Slate / Glass Slate Wall** — these were cut down to 54×54 and 108×108 in
  0.5.2, which left most frames empty, so blocks and walls had invisible holes. Rebuilt at
  288×270 and 468×180 from the same designs.
* **Backpack quick stash** — favourited items and backpacks are no longer stashed, stacking goes
  through tModLoader's stack rules, and it plays the grab sound.
* **Nyuu Test Kit** — drops sync in multiplayer; tooltip added. `icon_small.png` added.
